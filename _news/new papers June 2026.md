**Papers**

A nice crop of papers to report since my last update, with several now published and a few fresh preprints:
- [Reducing measurement error with adaptivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21283) with James Byrne and Noah Linden. Hot off the arXiv this morning! We show that adaptivity (feed-forward) is a genuine resource for lowering the error of measurement. This is James' first paper of his PhD!
- [The resource theory of interactive quantum instruments](https://doi.org/10.1103/dx6d-4kjy) with Chung-Yun Hsieh, Armin Tavakoli and Huan-Yu Ku, published in Phys. Rev. A.
- [Unspeakable coherence concentration](https://doi.org/10.1103/fj8w-v1bb) with Ben Stratton and Chung-Yun Hsieh, also published in Phys. Rev. A. This was Ben's final paper of his PhD.
- [All incompatible sets of measurements can generate nonlocality using quantum inputs](https://doi.org/10.1103/fgh1-gq8p) with Andres Ducuara, Patryk Lipka-Bartosik and Cristian Susa, published in  Phys. Rev. Lett. This is a long-resurrected project, which was great to final get out and have published!
- [Automated quantum circuit optimization with randomized replacements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15934) with Marcin Szyniszewski, Aleks Kissinger and Noah Linden.
- [Characterizing entanglement at finite temperature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15144) with Snigdh Sabharwal, Matthias Gohlke and Nic Shannon.
