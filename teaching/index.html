Quantum Mechanics

Current

From Classical to Modern Physics (2nd-year core unit) · University of Bristol · 2024/25 – present

The quantum mechanics part of the second-year core physics unit: the quantum description of a particle moving in one and three dimensions, from position, momentum and the uncertainty principle through to the harmonic oscillator, scattering and quantum mechanics in 3D. Taught as a flipped classroom, with pre-recorded videos embedded in the notes and weekly problem classes.

Textbook

Semidefinite Programming in Quantum Information Science

Paul Skrzypczyk and Daniel Cavalcanti · IOP Publishing, IOP Series in Quantum Technology · 2023

A graduate-level introduction to semidefinite programming and its use across quantum information science. After reviewing the theory of SDPs, the book shows how they can be used to address state, measurement and channel estimation and discrimination, entanglement detection and quantification, quantum distance measures, and measurement incompatibility. It assumes only basic linear algebra and quantum information.
